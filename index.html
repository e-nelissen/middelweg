Description

I am renting out my light, energy efficient apartment with an open view onto a sizeable city garden. It is located in a quiet and convivial street in the heart of Heverlee, at a few minutes by bike to the city center, imec, and large supermarkets. The forest starts at the end of the street and each Sunday the Heverlee market is around the corner.

The tenant must be willing to maintain the garden. In return, they will be rewarded with figs, strawberries, raspberries, and fresh herbs.

Electricity, water, or internet are not included. Smoking inside is not allowed.

A three months' deposit (&euro 3600) is required, to be put on a blocked bank account. The tenant is also required to have their own liability insurance (huurdersverzekering). At the start of the contract, we will ask an external party for a property condition report. This cost is shared between the tenant and the property owner.
